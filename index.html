Why this exists

The club is a dataset with feelings attached

Most United websites are built to win a click. This one is built to answer a question: is the last decade actually as bad as it feels, or does the history just make it feel that way? You cannot answer that with a paragraph. You answer it by putting 33 seasons on one axis and looking at the shape.

So the site stays narrow. It does not report news, rate transfers or pretend to know who should be manager. It keeps three things honest — who played, when they won, how the league went — and lets you interrogate them.
