Red Devils Data Desk — project description for the BiUH tracker
(the text to paste into "Description")

A data desk for Manchester United rather than a fan page: the numbers, made
sortable. It holds three datasets — all 33 Premier League seasons from 1992–93 to
2024–25 (finishing position, points, manager), the complete trophy cabinet (68
major honours, every winning year, filterable by type and counted by decade), and
24 players with their real appearance and goal totals. Three things are built on
top of them: an XI builder where you pick a position, choose an eligible player
and watch the combined totals change; an SVG chart that switches between points
and finishing position and between the Ferguson era and the twelve seasons since;
and a ten-question quiz where every correction links to the page that proves the
answer.

The interface follows the grammar of the Apple Sports match card: a deep
Manchester United red surface, the real club crest, 22 real player photographs
(every one free-licensed, with its author credited on the About page), large
tabular numbers and standings-style tables for the season and player data. It is
written in plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript — no framework, no build step, no back
end, no external libraries or fonts — and every figure is checkable against a
public record, which the About page documents along with the plan, the design
system and an honest account of how AI was used.
