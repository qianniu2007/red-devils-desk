The counting rules

Why 68, and not 69 or 71

The commonly quoted total is 68, and it counts only major honours: 20 league titles, 13 FA Cups, 6 League Cups, 21 Community Shields, 3 European Cups, and one each of the Cup Winners' Cup, European Super Cup, Europa League, Intercontinental Cup and Club World Cup.

Two things get argued about every time this number appears:

  • Shared shields. Four Charity/Community Shields were shared rather than won outright (1965, 1967, 1977, 1990). They are included here because the club counts them, and marked with a dashed border so you can subtract them yourself.
  • What counts as “major”. Second Division titles, the Manchester Cup and the Lancashire Cup are real trophies and are not in this total. Neither is the 1999 Treble as a separate item — it is three of the trophies above landing in ten days.

If you want the stricter number, remove the four shared shields: 64 outright.
